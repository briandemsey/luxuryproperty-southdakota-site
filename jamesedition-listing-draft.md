## Full description
A private residence on two acres above Spearfish, in the Northern Black Hills of South Dakota — from plans originally drawn by the late Laguna Beach architect Mark Singer, and completed in 2023. Offered furnished, in full, to a single qualified buyer.

Mark Singer (1948–2017) practiced from a studio in Laguna Beach for nearly thirty years. His body of work — residential, patient, and steadily awarded — drew on Frank Lloyd Wright, Richard Neutra, John Lautner, and Rudolph Schindler, turning the language of California modernism toward the specific coastal light in which he lived. The American Institute of Architects, Orange County chapter, counted his design awards at close to twenty.

The plans for 2111 Vantage Circle were drawn to those same principles, adapted for a very different site: a two-acre parcel on Lookout Mountain, above the small city of Spearfish, with west-facing exposure over a valley and treeline. Completed in 2023, the result is a Singer residence in an entirely different register — the coastal architect's grammar, executed for the interior west.

The house is arranged over two levels, roughly 3,700 square feet inclusive of a full walkout below, appointed for four bedrooms and two baths, with an open great room, dining, and kitchen configured to the western view. The exterior speaks in three materials — weathering flat metal, dimensional wood, and stone — under a standing-seam metal roofline that settles the building into the slope. A two-car garage, concrete-finished and heated, is integrated into the plan.

Interior finishes: poplar trim, quartz counters and fireplace facade, soft-close dovetailed maple cabinetry, hardwood in principal rooms, luxury vinyl plank in service areas. High-efficiency propane heat, central air, an owned two-tank water treatment system, and a tankless water heater. Shared water association; private septic.

The property is offered furnished, in full — including a commissioned dining table with a metal elk-form base, a six-foot metal photographic print above the great-room fireplace, an eight-foot live-edge wood slab wall sculpture, and a 1977 suite of eleven signed Corita Kent serigraphs.

South Dakota has no state income tax and has become a working domicile for a significant share of American family capital under its trust and tax framework. (This is a general description, not legal or tax advice — buyers should retain their own counsel.)
